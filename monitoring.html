How the Simulated Crash Detection Algorithm Operates

1. Sensor Telemetry Polling The system streams simulated tri-axial acceleration. Under normal riding conditions, the vertical vector approximates 1.0G (Earth gravity), while road vibration produces minor jitter.
2. Collision Shockwave Detection When "Simulate Accident" is invoked, a sharp spike exceeding 4.0G is generated concurrently with a sudden speed drop to 0 km/h, representing high-inertia collision impact.
3. Instant Triage & Geolocation The app captures browser coordinates (or fallback coordinates), synthesizes an audible emergency beacon, saves a timestamped incident record, and dispatches the alert to the dashboard.
